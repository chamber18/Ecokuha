Help & Support

FAQs

How do I post a collection? ▼

Tap the large "Post" button on the bottom navigation bar. Take or upload a photo of your recyclables, and our AI will help identify and estimate their value.

How is the value estimated? ▼

The AI estimates the type and weight of your items. The final value is determined by the junk shop after they weigh the items, based on their current pricing.

When do I get paid? ▼

Payment is typically processed by the junk shop through the app once the collection is completed and the items have been verified.
